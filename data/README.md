\# Data Access Notice



The full research dataset is not included in this public repository due to governance, ethical, and permission constraints.



This repository may include code, documentation, and selected demonstration outputs required to explain the GeoAI surveillance prototype. Full datasets, restricted operational files, or sensitive geospatial-health outputs are not distributed through this repository.



Access to full data may be granted only for authorised academic review, dissertation examination, or approved research purposes, subject to permission from the author and applicable data governance requirements.
